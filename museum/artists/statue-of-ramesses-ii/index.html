Statue of Ramesses II

ancient Egyptian red granite statue found in 1882 which is presented nowadays in the Grand Egyptian Museum of al-Giza, Egypt, c. 1250 BCE

The Statue of Ramesses II is a colossal 3,200-year-old figure of Ramesses II, depicting him standing. It is 11 metres (36 ft) tall and made from red granite, weighing about 83 short tons.

Colossal statue of Ramesses II at Karnak

Statue of Ramesses II in Karnak Temple in Luxor, Egypt. The Statue of Ramesses II is a colossal 3,200-year-old figure of Ramesses II, depicting him standing. It is 11 metres (36 ft) tall and made from red granite, weighing about 83 short tons.

In 1820, the statue was discovered, broken into six pieces, at Mit Rahina near ancient Memphis, Egypt, where it lay for several decades. History →
