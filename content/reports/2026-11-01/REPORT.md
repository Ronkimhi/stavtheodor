# stavtheodor.com page-by-page report, Nov 1 2026

## Bottom line

Google is showing the site much more (search impressions rose from 480 to 1,498 a week over the 5 weeks of Search Console data, and 127 pages are indexed, up from 108), but that reach is not turning into business: 8 key events in 12 months, all on the homepage and /contact/, and zero on the 15 money pages, 25 guides and 24 advisory articles. The work now is clicks and conversions, not more pages: better titles on pages Google already shows, push the Bergen County new-build pages, and make sure every "Write to Stav" click is counted.

## What's working

- Search reach is compounding: weekly impressions tripled in 5 weeks (480 to 1,498), and 19 more pages got indexed since Oct 23.
- Guides carry search: 2,333 of 4,873 impressions (48%) and 24 of 94 clicks. /guide/dental-office-decor/ is the best non-home page: 9 clicks, 406 impressions, position 9.3, 45s engagement.
- Local money pages rank on page 1 when they show: /art-consultant-tenafly-nj/ position 5.4 (4 clicks / 108), /art-curator-new-jersey/ 4.8 (4 / 90), /art-curator-new-york/ 6.4 (6 / 149, 108s engagement).
- AI assistants send the best traffic: chatgpt.com 40 sessions, 57.5% engaged, 1 key event (2.5% key event rate vs 0.9% for Direct).
- Leads are rising: 7 of the 8 key events in 12 months came in the last 28 days; generate_lead fired 6 times in d90 (3 on /, 3 on /contact/).
- Local designer searches already find the site: queries like "residential interior designers tenafly nj", "interior designers cresskill nj" and "interior design company alpine nj" show impressions.

## What's not working

- Zero key events outside / and /contact/ in 12 months. 15 money pages, 25 guides, 24 advisory articles, 16 projects: 0.
- CTR is low where impressions are high: /guide/art-sourcing-for-interior-designers/ 1.07% on 469, /guide/restaurant-wall-decor/ 0.61% on 326, /guide/hotel-artwork/ 1.06% on 283, /guide/how-to-hang-pictures/ 0.43% on 231. /advisory/ 0 clicks on 149, /hotels/ 0 on 94, /for-designers/ 0 on 90.
- The first ICP (Bergen County new builds) is invisible: /art-advisor-bergen-county/ 1 view and 46 impressions with 0 clicks in d90; /advisory/art-for-a-new-build-in-bergen-county/ 1 view and 0 impressions.
- Advisory articles are dead weight so far: 24 views in 90 days across 24 pages, 13 pages with 0 views, 4 not indexed.
- The museum dilutes the site: 144 pages (55% of the site), 126 of the 133 discovered-not-indexed URLs, 9 clicks, 0 key events. Many museum landings are Direct with 0 to 1 seconds of engagement (for example /museum/artists/polykleitos/ 11 sessions, 0.9s), which looks like bot traffic.
- Traffic quality is unfiltered: Direct is 661 of 1,118 sessions at 29.3% engagement; google / listing sent 27 sessions with 0 engaged; 63 landing sessions are "(not set)".
- Brand search is weak: "theodora" got 117 impressions at average position 11.9 and 0 clicks.
- /contact/ ranks at average position 54.6 on 76 impressions.
- 4 old Wix URLs still return 404 today: /press, /work-1/tel-aviv-apartment, /work-1/luxury-home-in-hertzliya-pituch, /work-1/st-regis-amman-jordan. (/contact and /blog, also on Google's 404 list, now resolve.)

## Data gaps

- GA4 does not export engagement rate per page in these reports; per page we show average engagement time per active user (pages) and per session (landing) instead.
- Bots and internal visits are not filtered in any GA4 number.
- GSC holds only 35 days (Sep 26 to Oct 30), so no 3 or 12 month search trend exists.
- Which GA4 events count as key events is not in the exports; per-page key events come from GA4's Key events column.
- Top queries per page were exported for 14 pages only (the homepage, /advisory/, /about/, /designers/, /art-curator-new-york/, /radar/, /museum/artists/tomb-of-the-leopards/ and 7 guides). Other pages show no query column.
- Two personal-name or private queries were removed from the public copies of the GSC query files.
