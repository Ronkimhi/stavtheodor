# Page JSON spec (content/pages/<slug>.json)

One JSON file per page. build-site-pages.py renders it into /<path>/index.html with the site's nav, style, language switch (English by default on every page since 2026-09-26, Hebrew when the visitor picks it, remembered site-wide), FAQ schema, breadcrumb schema and footer. Do not write HTML pages by hand.

```json
{
  "path": "advisory/what-does-an-art-advisor-cost",
  "section": "advisory",
  "kicker": "Art advisory",
  "nav_sub_en": "optional: the small line under the wordmark, like for designers",
  "nav_sub_he": "optional: its Hebrew twin",
  "title_en": "What does an art advisor cost?",
  "title_he": "כמה עולה יועצת אמנות?",
  "meta_description": "Under 160 characters. The direct answer, in English, for search and LLM snippets.",
  "lead_en": "One or two sentences that answer the question outright. Plain text, no HTML.",
  "lead_he": "Faithful Hebrew translation of the lead.",
  "before_after": "restaurants",
  "body_en": "<p>...</p><h2>...</h2><p>...</p>",
  "body_he": "<p>...</p><h2>...</h2><p>...</p>",
  "faq": [{"q_en": "...", "a_en": "...", "q_he": "...", "a_he": "..."}],
  "schema_type": "Service",
  "service_type": "Art advisory for private residences",
  "cta_en": "optional override of the closing call to action",
  "cta_he": "optional",
  "related": ["advisory/how-the-art-advisory-process-works", "projects/closter-new-jersey-new-construction"],
  "og_image": "/images/spaces/restaurants_og.jpg",
  "date": "2026-09-04"
}
```

Sections and paths: `advisory/<slug>` (schema_type Service or Article), `projects/<slug>` (schema_type CreativeWork or Article; a `hero_image` that is a real photograph of that same project, or, when there is none, `place_en` and `place_he`, the place name its typographic hero card shows; never another project's photo and never a before/after), `for-designers`, `for-brokers`, `for-advisors` (section "partners", schema_type Service), `guide/<slug>` (section "guide", schema_type Article), and the two local landing pages `art-curator-new-jersey`, `art-curator-new-york` (section "local", schema_type Service, 1,100 to 1,900 English words, "art curator" in title_en and lead_en, breadcrumb under /advisory/, listed first on the advisory hub and in the homepage Advisory rows).

The town and county pages (section "area", added 2026-09-29 for Ron's SEO brief, P1.2 and P1.3): `art-consultant-tenafly-nj` and `art-advisor-bergen-county`, and since 2026-09-30 `art-advisor-westchester-ny` (listed in `GENERATED_DIRS` of `tools/check_site.py`, like the other two, or the gates skip it). Their H1 is `title_en`, their `<title>` is `head_title` plus " · THEODORA", and they carry `og_title`, `breadcrumb` (a list of `[name_en, name_he, path]`, from `/` to the page itself: the visible trail above the H1 and the BreadcrumbList), `service` (the Service node: `name`, `serviceType`, `areaServed`, optional `audience`, `description`; provider is the entity by @id) and optional `cta_sub_en`/`cta_sub_he` (a line under the CTA, which on these pages also links /contact/). The FAQPage gets an `@id`, and a FAQ answer may carry links (the schema text is the answer with its tags stripped). 700 to 1,200 English words counted over the lead, the body and the questions; FAQ answers 10 to 110 words. `hero_image` may carry `w` and `h` (width and height on the img; a portrait image shows narrower).

`nav_sub_en` and `nav_sub_he` (optional, together, at most 32 characters each; added 2026-09-29 for /for-designers/ on Ron's approval) put a small uppercase line under the THEODORA wordmark in the nav ("for designers", "למעצבים"), the same `.wm-sub` as the buyer pages: hidden on the first fold, shown once the page's h1 has scrolled fully out of view, and kept for the rest of the page (`site_chrome.WM_SUB_H1_JS`, added by `build-site-pages.py` only on a page that has the fields).

The hero of an article page (advisory, local, partners, guide) is `before_after`, a key of `content/spaces.json` (added 2026-09-28): a pixel-aligned pair in `images/spaces/<key>_before.webp` and `_after.webp` (1800 px, with 1000 px twins `-1000.webp`) and a 1200 by 630 `<key>_og.jpg` for `og_image`. The spaces are AI-rendered with real catalog artworks placed on the wall, so the build labels every one "Proposal · how THEODORA would dress this space" and captions it from `spaces.json`, whose captions say "space", never "room". With JavaScript and motion allowed the figure pins for about 120vh while the after is brushed over the before (`js/before-after.js`, `.ba` in `css/theme.css`); otherwise it is a still pair. A page may use `hero_image` instead, for a real photograph. Give neighbouring pages different pairs.

Alt text comes in twins (2026-09-29): every `alt_en` (a `hero_image`, and each space in `content/spaces.json`) has an `alt_he` beside it, and the `hebrew` gate of `tools/check_site.py` fails the build when one is missing. The page writes the English in `alt` (English opens first) and the Hebrew in `data-alt-he` (`site_chrome.img_alt`); the language switch (`LANG_JS`) puts the Hebrew into `alt` while Hebrew is on and the English back when it is off. The before image of every pair has its own twin too, `before_alt_en` and `before_alt_he` in the pair's `content/spaces.json` entry (2026-10-06: the space with a bare wall plus one detail of it), read by `site_chrome.ba_before_alt()`; `site_chrome.BA_BEFORE_ALT` is only the fallback for a pair without them. Hebrew wording follows `content/glossary-he.json` (the preferred terms, the banned variants and the Latin words allowed inside Hebrew text), which the same gate enforces.

Optional fields: `head_title` (the page's `<title>` before " · THEODORA" when it should differ from the h1, `title_en`; at most 60 characters with the suffix; Ron's SEO brief of 2026-09-29, P1.4 and P1.5), `radar_posts` (a list of Art Radar slugs for the "From Art Radar" block every article page carries; without it the three newest posts show; every slug must exist in content/posts.html), `area_served` (a list of schema.org Place objects for Service pages; the default is `DEFAULT_AREA` in `build-site-pages.py`, the entity's areas: Tenafly, Bergen County, New Jersey, New York City and California; never Tel Aviv). An areaServed names markets the site targets, never a limit: THEODORA works online with clients anywhere, with no service boundary (Ron, 2026-09-30; `content/BRIEF.md` section 1). Never promise a "contact form": there is none. Point people to `stav@stavtheodor.com` and to "the contact details at the end of this page" linking `#contact` (Hebrew: "פרטי יצירת הקשר בסוף העמוד").

Body HTML rules: p, h2, h3, ul, ol, li, strong, em, a, blockquote, figure, img, figcaption only (a guide may also use the components under Guides below). Root relative image paths. Links to other pages of this set use `/advisory/<slug>/` style absolute paths. No inline styles, no classes, no scripts.

No internal notes in a page JSON. Every file under `content/` is served publicly exactly as it is in the repo (for example https://stavtheodor.com/content/pages/for-designers.json), so a key named `editor_note`, or any key starting with `_` or `note` (any case), at any depth, fails `tools/check_pages.py`, which `build-site-pages.py` runs on every page before writing anything, so `python3 build.py` stops. Internal notes and decisions go to the site owner's private system: `the-system-v8-ron/B-brain/06-deliverables/theodora-growth/decisions-for-stav.md` in the private ron-brain repo. There is no Stav review step and no open question is parked for her (Ron, 2026-09-30): a fact that does not trace to `content/BRIEF.md` or to Stav's 2026-09-30 answers is written as advice (the FACT-TRACE RULE at the top of `content/BRIEF.md`).

## Guides (Fable's design, 2026-09-29)

A guide page (section `guide`, path `guide/<slug>`) renders to its own template: the breadcrumb Home / Art advisory / the guide in place of the eyebrow (Hebrew דף הבית / ייעוץ אמנות) (BreadcrumbList THEODORA, Advisory, the guide), a tighter h1, the hero, the reading column as `.prose.guide` (text at 720 px, figures and tables up to 1000 px), then Questions, the CTA, Read next, and From Art Radar last. Styles: `css/theme.css` Part 5.

- Hero: a diagram, `hero_image` with `src` an SVG in `images/guides/<page>/`, `alt_en`/`alt_he`, `caption_en`/`caption_he`; it becomes Figure 1 and its width and height are read from the SVG when `w`/`h` are absent. A guide may keep a `before_after` hero instead (the ten questions guide does); it then sits among the advisory cards on /advisory/, not in the guides group. An SVG is never the link preview: give the guide an `og_image` (a 1200 by 630 JPEG).
- A guide may carry both (2026-10-01): `before_after` is then the page's hero, and `hero_image` stays the card thumbnail on /advisory/'s guides group and the guides strips. Its SVG is repeated in the body as a `figure.dia` (the build numbers it Figure 1 when it comes first), with the same alt and caption twins, so the diagram stays on the page. The guide's `og_image` is the pair's `/images/spaces/<key>_og.jpg`, and the page then says the preview's size and alt (`og:image:width`, `og:image:height`, `og:image:alt`). Guide pairs are keyed `<page-slug>_hero`.
- Photo figures in a guide body (companions and inline images, 2026-10-01): a plain `<figure>` (no class, so it is not numbered) placed directly after the diagram it illustrates, one or two images, each `<img src="/images/guides/<dir>/<file>-1000.webp" srcset="<file>-1000.webp 1000w, <file>.webp 1800w" sizes="(max-width: 767px) 100vw, 1000px" width="1800" height="1200" alt="...">`, and a figcaption that opens "Proposal ·" (Hebrew "הצעה ·"), because the build labels only hero pairs. A body image never reuses a file from `images/spaces/` (the hebrew gate expects that file's alt_he as data-alt-he): copy it into the guide's folder. Alt text (2026-10-06): 100 to 140 characters, the space and where the art hangs, then the art and its scale; distinct alts for an a/b pair; never claimed as a real photo or project; a page's primary keyword at most once across its alts. The caption after "Proposal ·" is one concrete sentence on what the image shows under the rule its section just gave. Every proposal WebP and og JPEG carries an XMP packet (IPTC DigitalSourceType compositeWithTrainedAlgorithmicMedia, dc:rights THEODORA, dc:description "AI-generated visualization featuring works from the THEODORA collection"), written by `ingest_guide_images.py`.
- Body components, allowed on guide bodies only (`tools/check_pages.py`): `<figure class="dia"><img src="/images/guides/<page>/<name>.svg" alt="..."><figcaption>caption</figcaption></figure>` (a diagram), `<figure class="tbl"><figcaption>caption</figcaption><div class="tbl-x"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">...</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><th scope="row">...</th><td>...</td></tr></tbody></table></div></figure>` (a table, four columns at most), `<aside class="cnote"><span class="cn-l">Curator's note</span><p>...</p></aside>` (Hebrew label הערת האוצרת; one or two per guide), `<div class="keynums"><div class="keynum"><span class="n">8 to 10<small>in</small></span><span class="l">from the top of the desk to the bottom of the frame</span></div></div>` (one to three numbers; ranges with "to", never a dash). Extra tags: div, span, small, aside, table, thead, tbody, tr, th, td; classes only dia, fig-n, cnote, cn-l, keynums, keynum, n, l, tbl, tbl-x; still no style and no script. Repeat every figure and table in `body_he` with the Hebrew alt and caption, same file.
- The build numbers the captions (Figure N / תרשים N after the hero, Table N / טבלה N, the same numbers in both twins; leave the number out of the JSON), adds width and height to diagram images, and gives every h2 an id (the slug of the English text; the Hebrew twin's h2 the same id plus `-he`).
- Optional fields: `crumb_en`/`crumb_he` (the breadcrumb's short name, at most 40 characters; default the title), `dek_en`/`dek_he` (the card's one line, at most 120 characters; default the lead's first sentence), `readnext_h2_en`/`readnext_h2_he` (Read next's heading, at most 90; default "More guides"), `byline_en`/`byline_he` (the visible line under the lead and the Article author; at most 160 characters; default "By Stav Theodor, art curator and advisor at THEODORA in Tenafly, New Jersey" / "מאת סתיו תאודור, אוצרת ויועצת אמנות ב-THEODORA בטנפליי, ניו ג'רזי"; the name links to /about/; the Article JSON-LD author is the site-wide Person with url /about/ and sameAs Instagram). Read next shows the `related` guides as cards (at most three, in order) and the other related pages as links under "Also on this site"; with no related guide, only the links.
- The `guides` field (optional, on any page but a guide; buyer variants have the same field, `content/VARIANT-SPEC.md`): 1 to 6 `guide/<slug>` paths of guide pages, in display order, rendered as a Guides strip (`#guides`, cards) after the reading column and before the questions. Heading `guides_heading_en`/`guides_heading_he` (at most 90 characters; required when `guides` is set, and it may be stored on a page before the guides exist; the safety-net default is "The answers in more detail" / התשובות, בפירוט). A guide that is not built is skipped.
- /advisory/ shows every guide with a diagram hero as its own group after the advisory cards (order: `GUIDE_ORDER` in `build-site-pages.py`, then newest first), and names every guide in its CollectionPage.
- Every guide sits in exactly one of the six categories of `GUIDE_BUCKETS` in `site_chrome.py` (2026-10-01): For the home, Offices and practices, Hospitality, For designers and builders, How advisory works, Hanging, lighting and care. A new guide is one line: its slug in the right tuple, where its place in the tuple is its place in the category. That list drives the nav's Guides panel (the guide shows as its `crumb_en`/`crumb_he`), the phone menu's category list and the groups and category bar on /guide/. The build stops on a guide in no category, in two, or a slug with no guide page.

Word limits read the prose only (2026-09-29, Ron): `tools/check_pages.py` leaves figure and table captions (`figcaption`, `caption`) out of the body count, so a guide's 900 to 1,400 words are its text without the captions.

Validate before you hand off: `python3 tools/check_pages.py content/pages/<slug>.json` must print OK.
