## What This Site Is

Art Radar is a curatorial newsletter and public archive published by THEODORA. It covers:
- Must-see exhibitions in New York City, New Jersey, and Tel Aviv
- Gallery openings and art fair coverage
- Art market news and auction results with curatorial analysis
- Collecting philosophy and art advisory guidance
- Museum access tips for NY/NJ audiences
- Profiles and tributes of major artists

Every post appears in Hebrew and in a full English translation on the same page.
The full archive, every post in order: https://stavtheodor.com/radar/
Structured data and metadata are in English.
A plain-text FAQ at https://stavtheodor.com/#faq answers common questions about
art curation for private residences, the advisory process, and where to find
art recommendations for New Jersey and New York.

## The Museum (Interactive)

The site also hosts The Museum at https://stavtheodor.com/museum/, a fully static,
interactive museum of art history: a zoomable timeline of 31 periods spanning
40,000 years (prehistoric caves, Mesopotamia, Egypt, the Aegean, Greece, the
Etruscans, Rome, then Medieval through Contemporary) with 141 artists, monuments,
and masterworks, placard biographies, and first-person walkable 3D galleries of
real artworks. All content is sourced from Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Wikimedia
Commons (text CC BY-SA 4.0, images public domain or Creative Commons with
attribution); nothing is AI-generated.

- Zoya Cherkassky: American Cooking, at Fort Gansevoort (Israeli artist Zoya Cherkassky, born in Kyiv and an immigrant to Israel in 1991, known for colorful, figurative, direct paintings of the life around her (people, family, food, class, culture and identity) that look everyday but carry a sharp, sometimes merciless read of society and stereotypes; after October 7 she moved with her family to the US and now lives and works in Long Island, again inside and outside a culture at once. Her new show American Cooking at Fort Gansevoort, 5 Ninth Avenue, New York, looks at American life through food, meals, family and social rituals, including paintings of Katz's Delicatessen and images born from an old American cookbook she found near her Long Island studio; on view September 16 to November 7, 2026, opening reception September 16, 6 to 8 PM): https://stavtheodor.com/radar/zoya-cherkassky-american-cooking/

The work uses ancient fertility figurines from the Kingdom of Judah, animated via motion capture with a figurine from the Lachish region as the artist's avatar, alongside the artist's own BRCA2 diagnosis, to hold together the personal and the national around motherhood; a post-October-7 work, The Mourners, was the only piece visible through the window of the closed pavilion in Venice): https://stavtheodor.com/radar/ruth-patir-jewish-museum/
- A September Gallery Walk in Tribeca: Four Shows to See (an independent curator's light walking route through four Tribeca gallery shows a few minutes' walk apart, all opening in early September 2026, startable from 11am on Broadway and relevant from September 10: Tony Cragg at Marian Goodman Gallery, 385 Broadway, large twisting bronze, wood, stone, glass and steel forms between abstraction and the human body, with a note on his permanent Points of View sculptures in the One Vanderbilt lobby, September 10 to October 24; Hew Locke's The Wanderers at P.P.O.W, 390 Broadway, ornamented sculptures, boats, historical figures such as Queen Victoria, and original colonial-era share certificates painted over to investigate empire and its afterlives, September 9 to October 24; Melissa Joseph's Casserole Culture at Charles Moffett, 394 Broadway, tender needle-felted scenes of family photographs and everyday domestic objects that reclaim textile craft as contemporary painting, September 9 to October 17; and Savdie / Soutine at James Cohan, 48 Walker Street, a two painter dialogue between Chaïm Soutine of the School of Paris and Brooklyn-based Colombian painter Ilana Savdie, both breaking down the body through color, distortion and texture, September 10 to October 24): https://stavtheodor.com/radar/four-tribeca-galleries-september-2026/
